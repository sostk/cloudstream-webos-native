## What's new in 0.9.1

### Fixes
- **Leaving the player no longer crashes the app.** Pressing Back during playback, or the next
  episode starting on its own, could close the app about half a second later. This happened on every
  title and plugin
  ([#42](https://github.com/sostk/cloudstream-webos-native/issues/42)).
- **The mouse pointer no longer makes buttons flicker.** With the Magic Remote resting over the
  top of the screen, focus could jump back and forth between two buttons many times a second.

### Looks
- **Home: the featured title is a banner, not the whole background.** Its artwork sits at the top of
  the page and scrolls away with it. The rows below are on the plain background, as in CloudStream's
  TV home.
- **The top bar turns to glass when you scroll.** Once a page scrolls, a strip of the same frosted
  glass as the tab bar fades in behind the profile, tabs and source button. Its lower edge fades into
  the page, and rows no longer show through the labels. This applies to Home, Search and Settings.
- **Long season lists scroll.** On a series with many seasons, the season row (and the episode-range
  row, such as 1-50 and 51-100) slides sideways to keep the focused chip on screen.

### Older TVs (webOS 3.9 and 4)
- The first video after starting the app is no longer oversized and cropped (LG B7).
- Videos without audio no longer stop after a few frames (LG B7).

### Logs
- The log from the previous run is kept as `events.prev.log`. A crash's log is not lost when the
  app is reopened before the logs are copied.
- Playback logs record more detail in the first 10 seconds of each video, and where playback had
  reached when it stopped.

### Known issues
- Some VidHide mirrors refuse TLS connections from certain networks; the plugin's other mirrors
  play.
- Some streams behind Cloudflare answer the TV's player with HTTP 403 while Android plays them
  ([#15](https://github.com/sostk/cloudstream-webos-native/issues/15)); this is being investigated.
