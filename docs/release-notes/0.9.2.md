## What's new in 0.9.2

### New
- **Torrent and magnet links play.** As in CloudStream, a TorrServer built from its source runs
  next to the app, on the TV only, and streams the chosen file to the player. Pieces are kept in
  memory, not written to the TV's storage. While a torrent starts, and on the control bar while it
  plays, the player shows seeds, peers and download speed.
- **Proxies per provider** (Settings → Network → Proxies). For each provider, its videos can be
  fetched directly, through a free public proxy in a country you choose (from ProxyScrape's list,
  tested before use and swapped when one fails), or through your own proxy server (HTTP, HTTPS or
  SOCKS, with an optional login that is never logged). Useful for services that only send video to
  viewers in their own country. Free proxies are slow and change often, and their operators can see
  which sites you reach.
- **"Because you watched …" on Home.** A row of recommendations for the show you watched last,
  from that show's own provider
  ([#37](https://github.com/sostk/cloudstream-webos-native/issues/37)).

### Fixes
- **The app no longer fills the TV's storage.** Every crash of the app or its plugin runtime left a
  memory dump of about 40 MB in the app's folder, and updating did not remove them. Dumps are now
  off, and any left behind are deleted when the app starts
  ([#36](https://github.com/sostk/cloudstream-webos-native/issues/36)). If your TV is already full,
  uninstall the app completely, then install this version.
- **The control bar hides sooner.** It hides 4 seconds after playback starts or after the last key
  press, whatever the plugin, and pressing Down on the bottom row hides it at once
  ([#44](https://github.com/sostk/cloudstream-webos-native/issues/44)).
- **Seeking on the progress bar moves in sensible steps:** 10 seconds at first, then 30, then 60 on
  long presses, instead of minutes at a time.
- **Encrypted live channels with several keys play.** Plugins that pass more than one ClearKey key
  in a link (as JioTV+ does) were refused as malformed.

### Known issues
- Torrents and proxies have been tested on webOS 6 (LG C1) only.
- Some services block viewers outside their country (JioTV answers HTTP 451); a proxy in that
  country is needed.
- Some VidHide mirrors refuse TLS connections from certain networks; the plugin's other mirrors
  play.
